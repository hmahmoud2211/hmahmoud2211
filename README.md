## About

I'm an AI/ML engineer focused on applying deep learning and predictive analytics to clinical and healthcare problems. My current work centers on **Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) prediction** from clinical datasets, alongside broader interests in digital twins, explainable AI, and decision-support systems for medicine.

- 🔬 Researching CKD risk prediction using real-world clinical data
- 🏥 Exploring digital twin architectures for healthcare applications
- 📊 Building interpretable, production-ready ML pipelines and BI dashboards
- 🌱 Continuously deepening my knowledge of deep learning, MLOps, and cloud infrastructure
